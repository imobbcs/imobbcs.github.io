About

From niche to mainstream.

I'm a crypto and fintech CMO based in Vienna, working across Europe. Today I'm Chief Growth Officer, CMO and Managing Director EU at Relai, Europe's leading licensed Bitcoin savings app, built on self-custody. I joined in 2021, right after the Series A, and I'm one of five decision-makers on the leadership team that sets company strategy, not only marketing strategy. I own how the company grows, competes and is perceived: brand, performance, lifecycle, ASO and SEO, product growth, analytics, PR and customer experience. The brand is the part of Relai people compliment first.

Before that, I was Head of Marketing at Bitpanda. I joined at around 100,000 users and 30 employees, built the marketing team from zero to seventeen, and left at 1.1 million+ users and 180+ employees.

The common thread: taking a financial product most people found intimidating and turning it into something they trust with their savings. At Relai, on a fraction of the category leaders' budgets: every €1 of marketing returning €5 in revenue, around 70% of signups organic, paid media as the accelerator rather than the engine, and search rankings next to the global exchanges.

In between, I was CMO at Robo Wunderkind, a consumer robotics brand for kids. Before fintech: a decade in media and entertainment, working with Universal Pictures, Xbox, PlayStation, Microsoft and Huawei.

In 2026, I was named one of the Fintech Marketing Hub's Top 30 Most Influential Fintech Marketers in Europe.
