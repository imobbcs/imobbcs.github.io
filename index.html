About

From niche to mainstream.

Chief Growth Officer, CMO and Managing Director EU at Relai, Europe's leading licensed Bitcoin savings app, built on self-custody. I joined in 2021, right after the Series A, and I'm one of five decision-makers on the leadership team that sets company strategy, not only marketing strategy. I own how the company grows, competes and is perceived: brand, performance, lifecycle, ASO and SEO, product growth, analytics, PR and customer experience. The brand is the part of Relai people compliment first.

Before that, I was Head of Marketing at Bitpanda. I joined at around 100,000 users and 30 employees, built the marketing team from zero to seventeen, and left at 1.1 million+ users and 180+ employees.

The common thread: taking a financial product most people found intimidating and turning it into something they trust with their savings. At Relai, on a fraction of the category leaders' budgets: around 70% of signups organic, paid media as the accelerator rather than the engine, and search rankings next to the global exchanges.

In between, I was CMO at Robo Wunderkind, a consumer robotics brand for kids. Before fintech: a decade in media and entertainment, working with Universal Pictures, Xbox, PlayStation, Microsoft and Huawei.

In 2026, I was named one of the Fintech Marketing Hub's Top 30 Most Influential Fintech Marketers in Europe.

I've led consumer crypto growth across Europe twice. As Head of Marketing at Bitpanda I scaled the platform from around 100,000 to over 1.1 million users. At Relai, where I'm Chief Growth Officer, CMO and Managing Director of the EU entity, I led the expansion from Switzerland into Germany, Italy, Spain and beyond, running growth in five languages; monthly active users grew 10x in two years. My approach: start with one beachhead market close to existing product-market fit (for Relai, Germany) and win it before expanding, keep one product but localise the plumbing (payments, IBANs, language and support), make search and AI search the front door, build organic growth and referral first with paid as the accelerator, build a brand people feel safe with, and keep measuring product-market fit with user surveys.
