About

I've done this before. Twice.

Chief Growth Officer and CMO at Relai, a MiCA-licensed, Bitcoin-only self-custody app scaling across DACH and Europe. I joined early, co-steer corporate strategy as part of the C-suite, and own everything that shapes how the company grows, competes, and is perceived.
